NOSTALGIA RACK - test build
===========================

Thanks for testing! Nostalgia Rack is a free hi-fi music player for Windows 10/11 (64-bit).

STARTING IT
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (for example your Documents). Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click NostalgiaRack.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's because this test build isn't signed yet.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

NOTHING IS INSTALLED
Everything it needs is in this folder. Your library and settings are kept in
%LocalAppData%\NostalgiaRack (paste that into File Explorer's address bar to see it).
To remove it completely, delete this folder and that one.

TRY THESE
- Drop a folder of music onto the playlist, double-click a song.
- The EQ deck, the VU meters, crossfade (gear key, top right).
- RADIO > TOP STATIONS, and PODCASTS > ADD FEED.
- Fold the decks with the little tabs, or press Ctrl+M for the slim strip.

FEEDBACK
Tell Yazan what you liked, what broke, and what confused you. Screenshots help a lot.
If an error box appears, press Ctrl+C on it and paste the text in your message.

The compressor knobs on the EQ deck don't work yet; they're coming in the next version.
